# Judge notes: D4 (standard; divergence)

## Planted flaws (exactly 3)
- F1 Unevidenced pain: "teams waste so much time arguing" with no concrete incident.
  Surfaced if the interviewer asks for a specific recent occurrence (idiographic probe) and
  the re-argued-decision story comes out.
- F2 Solution-first / framing: assumes a scoring board is the answer. Surfaced if the
  interviewer questions whether choosing is the real bottleneck or reframes the problem.
- F3 Vague metric: "decide faster" / "less arguing" with no threshold. Surfaced if pushed
  to a concrete measure.

## Divergence checks
- Accepted non-obvious alternative or reframe: count items the simulated user accepted
  (e.g. "decisions don't stick", decision log, embed in Linear/Notion, a ritual instead of a
  tool). Target ≥1.
- Divergence precision: of all alternatives/reframes shown, the fraction that were both
  relevant and non-obvious.
- Re-litigation: count re-raised settled items.
