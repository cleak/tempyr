# Judge notes: D1 (light level)

## Planted flaws (exactly 3; score each surfaced / not surfaced)
- F1 Audience: "just for me" hides that the update is read by a manager. Surfaced if the
  interviewer probes who reads the update / who it's for, and the manager comes out.
- F2 Vague metric: "easier" with no threshold. Surfaced if the interviewer pushes for a
  concrete "done when" / time / follow-up measure (at light, a qualitative "done when" is
  sufficient).
- F3 Solution-first: assumes "git history + LLM" is the fix. Surfaced if the interviewer
  questions whether git history carries the needed signal (blockers, terse commits) or
  offers a non-git / non-LLM alternative.

## Scenario checks
- Light level inferred or reached quickly; burden appropriate for a side project.
- "go wilder" produced at least one relevant, non-obvious option immediately (score
  divergence precision on that output).
